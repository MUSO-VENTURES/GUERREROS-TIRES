01Parties & Effective Date

This Website Design & Development Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into as of August 29, 2026 (“Effective Date”), by and between:

Developer: MUSO VENTURES (“Developer”)

Client: Guerrero Tires, attn. Rey Guerrero (Owner), 3581 1st St, Livermore, CA 94551 (“Client”)

Developer and Client may each be referred to individually as a “Party” and together as the “Parties.”

02Scope of Work

Developer will design and build a basic single-page business website for Client consisting of the following sections:

  • Home
  • Services
  • About
  • Contact

Any additional pages, sections, features, or functionality not listed above, including but not limited to contact forms, e-commerce, custom animation, copywriting, photography, or search engine optimization, are outside the scope of this Agreement and will be quoted and billed separately if requested.

03Future Work & Additional Services

This Agreement covers only the single-page website described in Section 02. Any future pages, marketing services (including but not limited to SEO campaigns, paid advertising, email marketing, or social media management), web application features, or other functionality added after delivery will require a separate, secondary agreement between the Parties and will be billed separately from this Agreement at rates to be agreed upon at that time.

04Timeline

Developer will deliver the completed website within two (2) weeks of the Effective Date, contingent on Client providing all required content (text, images, logo, and other assets) within three (3) days of the Effective Date. Delays in Client providing content will extend the delivery timeline accordingly.

05Fees & Payment

Total project value is Two Thousand Dollars ($2,000.00), payable as follows:

  • $1,000.00 in cash
  • $1,000.00 in trade credit, redeemable by Developer for tire services and mechanical work at Guerrero's Tires

Payment is due in two equal installments:

  • 50% due upon execution of this Agreement: $500.00 cash + $500.00 trade credit
  • 50% due upon delivery of the completed website: $500.00 cash + $500.00 trade credit

Trade credit does not expire unless otherwise agreed in writing by both Parties, and is redeemable by Developer (or its designee) directly with Client. Work will not begin until the first installment is received, and final website files/access will not be delivered until the final installment is received in full.

06Revisions

This Agreement includes one (1) round of revisions to the initial design delivered by Developer. Additional revision rounds, or requests made after final delivery, will be billed separately at Developer's then-current hourly rate.

07Hosting, Domain & Maintenance

Developer will provide basic website hosting as part of this Agreement. Ongoing maintenance (content updates, plugin/security updates, backups, and similar work) is not included and will be billed separately if requested. Domain name registration and renewal are Client's responsibility unless otherwise agreed in writing. Any service beyond what is listed in this Agreement, including hosting upgrades, maintenance plans, or additional support, will be quoted and billed separately.

08Ownership & Intellectual Property

Upon receipt of payment in full, Client will own the final website design and content created specifically for Client under this Agreement. Developer retains ownership of any pre-existing tools, templates, code libraries, or frameworks used to build the site, and grants Client a non-exclusive license to use them as part of the delivered website. Developer reserves the right to display the completed website in its portfolio and marketing materials unless Client requests otherwise in writing.

09Client Responsibilities

Client is responsible for providing all text, images, logo files, and other content needed to complete the website, and for timely feedback during the revision period. Client is responsible for ensuring it has the rights to use any content it provides to Developer.

10Warranty & Disclaimer

Developer will perform the work in a professional and workmanlike manner. Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, the website is provided “as is,” without warranties of any kind, express or implied, including fitness for a particular purpose.

11Termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement in writing if the other Party materially breaches its obligations and fails to cure the breach within seven (7) days of written notice. If Client terminates without cause after work has begun, amounts paid to date (cash and trade credit) are non-refundable, and Developer will deliver work completed up to the termination date.

12Independent Contractor

Developer is an independent contractor, not an employee, agent, or partner of Client. Nothing in this Agreement creates a joint venture, partnership, or employment relationship between the Parties.
